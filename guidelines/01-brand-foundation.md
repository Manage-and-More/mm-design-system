# Brand foundation and architecture

## In one paragraph

Manage and More (M&M) is an **Endorsed Brand** of **UnternehmerTUM** (Ring 3 in the UnternehmerTUM brand architecture). It has its **own symbol and wordmark, its own colour (blue `#00A2CD`), its own website and social channels**, and it shows that it belongs to UnternehmerTUM through the **BY UNTERNEHMERTUM logo label**. It uses the **UnternehmerTUM corporate typefaces** (Sharp Sans, or Work Sans as the free alternative).

## The four rings (PDF p.5)

| Ring | Type | Logo | Colour | Type | Layout | Label | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Umbrella brand (UnternehmerTUM) | UnternehmerTUM | 100% UnternehmerTUM CD | Corporate | Corporate | n/a | UnternehmerTUM website and social |
| 2 | Aligned brand | "U" + wordmark | Own | Corporate (Sharp Sans / Work Sans) | Corporate | BY UNTERNEHMERTUM | CD-conform subdomain, own social |
| **3** | **Endorsed brand, e.g. Manage and More** | **Own symbol + wordmark** | **Own** | **Corporate (Sharp Sans / Work Sans)** | **Own or corporate** | **BY UNTERNEHMERTUM** | **Own website, own social** |
| 4 | Cooperation | Fully independent | Independent | Independent | Independent | optional | Independent |

Definitions (PDF p.4):

- **Aligned brand** (branded house): communicates under the umbrella brand, visibly tied to it (like Nivea / Nivea Men).
- **Endorsed brand** (house of brands): communicates independently; the umbrella brand acts as a quality seal that builds awareness and trust (like KitKat with Nestlé).

## Sub-brand rules that apply to Manage and More (PDF p.6-7)

- A sub-brand has a clearly defined offer, audience and market, and its own responsible team. It is approved by UnternehmerTUM management.
- **Formats and offers inside Manage and More do not get their own logos** (events can be the exception). They are communicated by Manage and More itself and may be distinguished by colour, but they get no extra online channels.
  - Wrong: a flyer where each programme format has its own logo.
  - Right: one Manage and More logo; formats are listed by name, optionally colour-coded.

## What this means in practice

1. Always lead with the Manage and More logo; add the BY UNTERNEHMERTUM label (and, where useful, the ENTREPRENEURIAL EDUCATION descriptor) to show the endorsement. See [03-endorsement-labels.md](03-endorsement-labels.md).
2. Use M&M blue as the signature colour, never UnternehmerTUM blue for the label.
3. Use Sharp Sans (Manage and More holds a licence) for everything designed, with Work Sans as the free fallback; Arial only for e-mail and office documents.
4. Do not invent sub-logos for programmes, tracks or events without an explicit decision by the brand owner.

## Name usage

- Written name: **Manage and More** (the PDF and website write "and"; the logo sets it in capitals as "MANAGE AND MORE").
- Short form: **M&M** is used informally; prefer the full name in titles and first mentions. *(Inferred from common use; the PDF does not define a short form.)*
