# Brand v2

**Quiet page, one living drawing.** Brand v1 (the programme website) is loud by design: Extrabold 50px headlines, alternating black and white bands, photo heroes. Brand v2 is the language for the team's *tech products*: near-monochrome surfaces, Apple-like spacing and type, and a single colour, Manage and More blue, carried mostly by the lit stroke of a **hairline figure**, an isometric line drawing that answers the pointer.

It is still Manage and More: the same blue, Sharp Sans / Work Sans, the BY UNTERNEHMERTUM label and lock-up, the arrow link, the top-rule stat, the two-tone heading (once per page), one flat blue section per page, and the brand's own illustration rule ("line art, one thin stroke, one brand colour") carried into motion.

See it: `cd playground && pnpm dev`, variant **Brand v2**, page *Landing page* (Compare it with Brand v1). Framework-free: [`examples/brand-v2.html`](../examples/brand-v2.html) with [`examples/brand-v2.css`](../examples/brand-v2.css).

## What changes against Brand v1

| Brand v1 | Brand v2 |
|---|---|
| Brand blue `#00A2CD` as large surfaces | **One blue, the Brand v2 blue `#0071E3`** (`#2997FF` on dark), the system blue of Apple-style UI. It replaces the brand blue everywhere in this language: the band, the figures' lit stroke, the logo swirl, and as the **action colour** (`--mm-color-semantic-action`): links, action pills, status chips, the arrow sticker, a phrase in a heading. Plus **one flat blue band per page** (white text). |
| Yellow `#FFED00` as the energetic accent | **No accent colour.** Brand v2 is blue, black, white and the greys. Yellow is retired entirely (illustrations sit on the parchment tile, chart series use the blue tint `#BFDBFF`). Campaigns and merch that want yellow are Brand v1 |
| Alternating black / white sections with hairline rules | **Full-bleed tiles**: light (white), parchment (`#ECECEF`, one step below the canvas) and dark (black). The colour change is the divider; no rules between sections. The blue band keeps square corners. |
| Photo hero with black shade | Text left, the hairline figure on a white **plate with the product shadow** right; no photo |
| Headings 50px, no tracking, outline + solid everywhere | Fluid display at `-0.02em`, headings at `-0.015em`; the outline line once per page, painted under a surface-coloured fill (`paint-order`) so glyph contours never show |
| Filled sticker arrow link | Kept, in the action colour, scaling 1.08 on a spring. **Pill = action:** primary black pill, blue action pill with arrow, quiet hairline pill |
| Bordered cards, 0.75rem | **Canvas-toned cards lifted by a soft layered shadow, never a border.** Radius grammar xs 5 / sm 8 / md 11 / lg 18 / xl 22 / pill |
| Flat motion, no shadows | Two shadows only: `shadow.card` (layered lift, cards and plates) and `shadow.product` (the hero figure). Buttons and text stay flat. Springs (`linear()`) for pointer-driven transforms. |
| Eyebrow counts above headings | **No kicker labels.** Nothing sits above a heading; status goes into a chip. |

## Rules

1. **One drawing per view carries the colour.** The hero figure sits on a plate with the product shadow and plays its tour until the pointer arrives.
2. **One blue, no accent.** Blue, black, white, greys; charts in blue, black, greys and the blue tint `#BFDBFF`.
3. **The blue acts.** Links, action pills, live chips, the arrow sticker and at most one phrase per heading are blue. Never a card background, never a gradient. On the band everything is white.
4. **Pill = action.** Only buttons and status chips are pills. Cards use lg, large containers xl, inputs md, inline chips sm.
5. **Hierarchy from tiles and shadow, not borders.** Sections are canvas or a full-bleed tile; the colour change divides them. Cards lift on `shadow.card`; hairlines appear only inside components (card foot, FAQ rows, stat rule).
6. **No kicker labels.** Nothing sits above a heading. Status is a chip, counts are in the copy.
7. **Type:** Sharp Sans 500 / 800, Work Sans fallback; display and headings with negative tracking, body at 0; the outline line once, in the hero. No italic, left-aligned.
8. **Motion:** respond, don't perform. Hover and press use the spring curve; the nav underline wipes in from the left (as on manageandmore.de); nothing animates on scroll; figures sleep offscreen and stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
9. **Hairline figures:** `--hairline-plate` must equal the surface the figure sits on (`.v2-figure--plate / --framed / --raised / --surface` set it). Empty states use a library figure at 200px above a heading and one action.
10. Everything else follows the brand: contrast rules of [04-color.md](04-color.md), the logo and label files, the endorsement in the footer, the voice of [12-voice-and-tone.md](12-voice-and-tone.md).

## Assets

[`assets/v2/`](../assets/v2/README.md) mirrors `assets/` for every file that carries the blue: logos and symbol, labels and lock-ups, illustrations, infographics, the blue icon PNGs and the tint examples, in the same sub-folders with the same file names, so a project switches brand by switching the folder. It is generated by `scripts/build_v2_assets.py` and catalogued with `brand: "v2"`. Everything without blue (black/white logos, `currentColor` icons and UI glyphs, photos, fonts) is shared from `assets/`.

## Components

`examples/brand-v2.css` (`.v2-*`), React versions in `playground/src/variants/brand-v2/components/`: header (underline wipe, one pill CTA), hero (figure plate), display, pills (primary, action, quiet, small), arrow link, text link, chips (live, sm), section (canvas or tile) + section head, card (figure, pictogram or number; chip on top, hairline foot; linked cards lift), audience tabs, stat row, blue band, FAQ (native `details`, animated `::details-content`), empty state, footer, and `Figure` (library or custom, with read-out).

## Figures

Library (`@lucasmarkes/hairline`, MIT, 63 kB for all 33, tree-shakeable): **Terminal** (organisers), **Lockers**, **Riffle**, **Elevator** (feature cards), **Plot**, **Query**, **Drawer** (empty states), Terrain, Hub, Stack. React: `@lucasmarkes/hairline/react`; plain DOM: `terminal(el, { intensity, theme, onRead, play })`. Colour comes from the six `--hairline-*` custom properties, which this language sets from its tokens.

Custom, built with the `hairline-create` skill (`.agents/skills/hairline-create/`, sources in `playground/hairline/figures/`, bundled for pages without a bundler by `node scripts/build_figures.mjs` into `examples/hairline-figures.js`). Each passed the skill's validator and the eight-picture look in a browser.

| Figure | For | Object, gesture, read-out | Leans on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bench** | everyone (hero) | Two desks on trestles with a walkway between, three spaced team stations each (mat, laptop, mug). The pointer picks the nearest station: its lid opens to 112°, its mat lifts, desk-mates less, staggered 45ms along the desk. `team 03`. | hit, order, rest, radius |
| **podium** | judges | Seven plinths on a stage in a slight arc. The pointer's height is a score line; plinths rise toward it, falling off with distance. `rank 1`…`rank 7`. Rest: a 3-1-2 podium. | hit, reach, rest, radius |
| **hours** | participants | Eight glass chambers with necks, 48 beads always falling. Hovering slows time to a tenth; the chamber and bead under the pointer are bright. `h 31`. Ambient; sleeps offscreen; still under reduced motion. | hit, accent, cost, clock |

To change one: edit its `.js`, run `node .agents/skills/hairline-create/look.mjs <figure>.js --answer … --edge …` from `playground/hairline/out/<name>/` until it exits 0, read the sheet, then `node scripts/build_figures.mjs`.

## Working on it

- Playground: `cd playground && pnpm dev`, variant **Brand v2**; `pnpm shot brand-v2 <page> [--vp 390] [--mode dark]` for a screenshot with console errors. The variant retires `color.brand.yellow` from its Foundations pages with `hideTokens` in `variant.ts`.
- Tokens: edit `playground/src/variants/brand-v2/tokens.json`, then `python3 scripts/build_tokens.py` (writes `tokens/build/variants/brand-v2.css`).
- Assets: edit the source in `assets/`, then `python3 scripts/build_v2_assets.py`; never edit `assets/v2/` by hand.
- Figures: see above; after a change, `node scripts/build_figures.mjs`.
- The standalone page: `examples/brand-v2.html` opens from disk; the library figures need a network connection.

## Deviations from the brand, logged

| Deviation | Why | Logged |
|---|---|---|
| Screen greys `#F5F5F7`, `#ECECEF`, `#0B0B0C`, `#151517` outside the palette | A quiet page needs a canvas that is neither paper white nor `#E3E3E3`; near-black instead of pure black keeps dark mode from glowing | [sources-and-discrepancies.md](sources-and-discrepancies.md#decisions-log) |
| Negative tracking on display and headings | Apple's size-specific tracking; Sharp Sans at 60px+ sets too loose | same |
| Two shadows: the layered card lift and the product shadow | Hierarchy without borders; buttons and text stay flat | same |
| Translucent header | Content scrolls under the chrome; solid under reduced transparency | same |
| The blue is `#0071E3` / `#2997FF`, not `#00A2CD`, everywhere in this language, logo included (`assets/v2/`) | The AA shade of the brand blue is a dull teal at small sizes, the brand blue itself fails AA for text, and two blues on one page collide. Text on the band is white | same |
| No kicker labels (all languages) | A heading carries its own message; status is a chip | same |
