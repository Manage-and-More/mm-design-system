# Voice and tone

> The 2020 guideline PDF defines no verbal identity. This page records how the live website writes, so new copy sounds like the brand.

## Basics

- **Language:** English only. The programme "is conducted entirely in English". The PDF itself is German.
- **Name:** "Manage and More" in running text and titles. The short form "MM" appears in compound names ("MM Alumni Community", "MM Alumni Association"). Avoid "Manage & More" in body copy (the website uses it once, on the Program page). The LinkedIn handle reads "Manage&More by UnternehmerTUM".
- **Umbrella:** "UnternehmerTUM", described as "Europe's #1 startup hub".
- **Positioning line:** "Enabling entrepreneurial leaders of tomorrow." Sub-line: "People who build and scale companies."
- **Description:** "Manage and More is the flagship entrepreneurship program for top talent in Munich, a central part of Europe's #1 startup hub, UnternehmerTUM."

## Headline patterns

### 1. Imperative triplets (solid only)

"Connect. Grow. Belong." · "Learn. Lead. Launch." · "Build. Scale. Succeed."

### 2. Two-part headline: setup (outline) + payoff (solid)

The two-tone heading is a copy pattern too. The outlined first line sets the scene, often ending in a colon; the solid second line names the thing.

| Outline (setup) | Solid (payoff) |
|---|---|
| ENABLING ENTREPRENEURIAL LEADERS OF TOMORROW | People who build and scale companies. |
| The Proven Framework to | Build and Scale. |
| The Most Important Part: | Get to Know the People |
| From foundation | to launch. |
| The heart of it all: | The Start-Up Project |
| Take ownership: | The Areas |
| Broaden your horizons: | International Opportunities |
| Explore the scene: | Startup Tours |
| Where our culture is built: | Events |
| Learn from the best: | Events with Partner Companies |
| And more: | Optional elements |
| Find your tribe. | Get to know us! |
| Requirements / Documents / Process | for Application / Required for the Application / of Application |
| We are excited to | meet YOU! |
| Meet Our | Scholars / Alumni |

Keep the outline part short (2-5 words); the solid part carries the meaning on its own.

### 3. Plain questions and statements

"What's it about?" · "Ready to join Manage and More?" · "Backed by Europe's #1 Startup Hub." · "How we stay in touch"

## Calls to action

Short, verb-first, sentence or title case, no "click here". Used as [arrow links](13-web-components.md#arrow-link-primary-call-to-action):

"Meet the Community" · "Explore the program" · "Feel the impact" · "Discover UnternehmerTUM" · "Join an Open Event" · "Join an upcoming Event" · "Connect with us!" · "Get to know us" · "Apply Now"

## Body copy

- **Speak to the reader as "you", the community as "we".** "From day one you are part of a tight-knit circle…"
- **Lead with the benefit**, then the mechanism. "You are not joining a standalone program. You are plugging into an entire ecosystem…"
- **Bold the key phrase** of a paragraph (Extrabold): "**flagship entrepreneurship program**", "**turn bold ideas into scalable ventures**", "**No idea? No problem.**"
- **Proof through numbers and names:** "> 2.3 B. $ capital raised", "10+ alumni-founded VC funds", "260+ founded startups", "#1 innovation ecosystem, 3 years running", alumni startup names.
- **Startup and VC vocabulary**, used with confidence: scalable ventures, co-founders, market gaps, "up to Series A", "From makerspace to UVC. From first prototype to first million."
- **Warm community tone:** "Find your tribe.", "Our beloved program management", "Events for fun", "lifelong friendships".
- Step lists are concrete: "Step 1: Join an Open Event to learn more. Step 2: Apply Now… Step 3: Get accepted…"

## Writing rules

1. Short, active sentences; lead with the benefit.
2. Headlines 2-6 words. Triplets or the setup/payoff pair. A full stop is allowed.
3. Back every claim with a number, a name or a concrete example.
4. One bold key phrase per paragraph at most.
5. Spell "startup" as one word in running text. The programme element is "Start-Up Project" (title case, hyphenated).
6. Dates written out with the time zone for deadlines: "Sun, June 7th, 2026, 23:59:59 (CEST)".
